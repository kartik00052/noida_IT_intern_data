How to read this data

Every record in this build is an unverified lead. Park coordinates are approximate OpenStreetMap geocodes. No tenant count, ranking score, technology stack, phone number or live job opening was invented. Use the evidence links and the source register to verify before applying.

Scoring method (from the brief): verified technology companies 25, MNC/GCC presence 15, product/fintech presence 15, software and R&D relevance 15, diversity 10, infrastructure 10, internship/employment relevance 10. Scores are null here because verification evidence was unavailable in this environment.
